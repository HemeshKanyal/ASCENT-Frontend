09 Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. Training, food and progress all work offline without one. You only need an account for Crew — friends, clubs and leaderboards.

Where does my data go?

It stays on your phone. Posts you choose to share go to the Crew server. If you log a meal by photo, that photo is sent to an AI service to estimate the food — scanning, searching and typing stay on your phone.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. Android gets the app; on iPhone (or any computer) use the web app in your browser.

Why does Android warn me when I install it?

The app is downloaded from here rather than the Play Store, so Android asks you to allow installs from this source once. That's normal for apps outside the store.

Is this medical advice?

No. ASCENT is a training tool. It adjusts to conditions you tell it about, but your doctor's instructions always come first.
